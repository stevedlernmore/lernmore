01 — The Problem

You already know something is wrong.

The data exists. The spreadsheets exist. Someone in the building probably has a theory.

Every input: tariffs, rebates, returns, rep fees, lives in a different system. The true margin exists somewhere across a dozen spreadsheets that only one person understands.

By the time you have a clean view of what's happening, the sourcing bet is placed, the price is set, and the quarter is in motion.

The PE firm wants a specific, defensible number. Not a range. Not "we think." You have two weeks to produce it from three systems that don't talk to each other.
